In order to spill out this legendary and super lasting fan-shaped foam sprayer . Let's have a look the instruction.

First, let's first understand the structure of the fan-shaped foam sprayer. It has fan nozzle, 304 stainless steel pump rod, Teflon piston solvent straw, air pressure pump, foam sponge and conical bottle combined together.

## The operation principle of Super long-lasting foam sprayer is:

1. Use 304 stainless steel pumping rod to make air into the bottle and pressurize the car wash detergent in the bottle.

2. Press the air pressure pump to make air into the bottle, the compressed air will then push the car wash detergent to the solvent suction tube.

![](/uploads/article-msg66-04.jpg)

![](/uploads/article-msg66-05.jpg)

3. After the car wash detergent passed through the foam sponge and the fan-shaped nozzle, a continuous atomized foam is sprayed out.

Yes! It's just so easy and simple to spray a super long-lasting foam!

## Tips to share:

- The ratio of car wash detergent to air is 2:1, please reserve the space to facilitate air compression.

- The more air that pumps into the bottle, the longer it takes to spray the foam.

- If the pump rod has been unable to pressurize, please stop pumping action in case to cause excessive air pressure to break the bottle.

- After the air pressure is run out, as long as it is inflatable again, you can spray fine foam for another 30 seconds. It can save time and efficient without hurting the car body.

- Please tighten the sprayer bottle then do the pumping actions otherwise the hard pressurization operation were in vain!
