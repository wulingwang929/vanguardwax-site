Modern people have a busy and hardworking life who usually doesn’t have much time to take care of their cars after get off work. Sometimes, we are rushing to go on a date and meeting customers then found that our car parked outdoors for a night that became dirty. Due to time constraint, it was not possible to clean the car properly. Therefore, we especially disclosed the secret car wash method for you. We are hoping to help car wash DIY players who do not have much time to wash their cars and bring their bright shine car back.

## Vanguardwax - Super Magic Water Repellent QUICK AUQA Coating

First of all, clean your car thoroughly with water and do not rush back to dry. Then come up with Vanguard water repellent QUICK AQUA spray coating on your car paint and the glass. After that, rinse it then you can see splashing coating effects with three-dimensional full water drop on the car body which is just like waxed. It’s not only easy operating and time saving, but also increase the brightness of your car paint with protective effect.

## Formula 1 - "Shining" Long-acting Tire Gloss Oil

After cleaning the car body, you might feel weird about when the tires are black and dirty. At this time, the "shining" long-acting tire gloss oil of Formula1 can be used. Just simply sprayed it the tire brush and the tire sidewall. Then wait for the penetration time about 3 to 5 minutes that can strengthen the gloss, elasticity, prevent cracking and make the effect to achieve the maximum.

If you only complete the above steps, it is not enough! Although the appearance looks very shining, the small details are still unsatisfactory. We are going to share two tips for you. The first tip is the inside of the door handle from the front passenger seat is easy to get dust even though the appearance is very clean at first glance. It will be embarrassing! Therefore, please do NOT ignore the cleaning of the door handles.

![](/uploads/article-msg62-07.jpg)

![](/uploads/article-msg62-08.jpg)

And the next step is to clean the foot pads in the car. There are a lot of people would ignore this place. Every time when they enter the car, they find the foot pads are very dirty. If there is drink overturning, children spilling milk, eating food and so on, you can use Formula1 carpet & upholstery "quick-drying" cleaning agent to clean. As long as it sprayed directly on the dirty place and wait for 10 seconds for decomposition, then with a soft brush to help removing stubborn dirt and finally dry with wet microfiber cloth.

If it is only dust or gravel, it can also be cleaned by a car vacuum cleaner.

![](/uploads/article-msg62-09.jpg)

![](/uploads/article-msg62-10.jpg)

Can try to find out the corner with no one there, use foot pad to hit the wall for dropping the dust. Please do NOT forget to look at the wind direction first so that your face may not with dust when operating.

These secret tips can make your car maintain beauty in a short period of time. We highly recommend you clean your car thoroughly and do car maintenance when you are flexible. Even though it will take more time, you must go out with a shining car.
