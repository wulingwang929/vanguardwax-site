**Car oriented, customer first.** To many people a car is just a way to get around; to car lovers it is a partner that deserves care.

In 1978, founder and Chairman Hsieh started KYOEI TAIWAN CORP., riding a motorcycle as both salesman and delivery driver to bring car care products to customers. Guided by the values of integrity, quality and innovation, he visited car care brands around the world and introduced products such as FORMULA 1 and MILITEC-1 from the USA, and AION and S.T. (エステー) from Japan, to Taiwan.

Beyond distribution, KYOEI developed its own brand, **VANGUARD**, drawing on technical cooperation between Taiwan and Japan to create car care chemicals suited to Taiwan's climate. The Glass Scale Remover paste and Porcelain Glaze car wax are customer favourites, and the patented CLAY-U clay mitt is now sold in many countries.

In 2010, Chairman Hsieh handed leadership to General Manager Zhou, who had worked at the company for more than ten years. Today KYOEI continues to grow its online and overseas channels, bringing quality, good-value car care products to more drivers.
